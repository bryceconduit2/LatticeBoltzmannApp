# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 14, 2026  

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Fluid Sandbox (the "App"), developed and maintained by Bryce Conduit ("we", "us", or "our"). 

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the App.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

> **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data.**

* **No Personal Data Collected:** The App does not ask for, request, access, or record personal information such as your name, email address, IP address, device IDs, or location.
* **No Analytics or Crash Reporting:** The App does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, tracking tools, or automated crash-reporting frameworks.
* **No Advertisements:** The App contains no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, or ad networks.
* **User Inputs & Local Storage:** Any choices, inputs, or calculations you make within the App are processed locally on your device in real time. This data never leaves your device and is not saved to any external servers.

---

### 2. Permissions

The App operates entirely locally and does not request or require sensitive device permissions (such as access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage).

---

### 3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because the App does not collect, request, or share any data from any user, it is fully compliant with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K)**. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party APIs, SDKs, or external services that collect user data.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Since the App does not collect user contact information, you are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
